Support

Help, honestly.

A dedicated support route (somewhere you can write to and get a reply) is still an open item for launch. Until it exists, this page says plainly what there is and what there isn’t.

What exists today

  • In the app. Settings ▸ Privacy lists the hosts Oriel may contact and every request made since launch. Settings ▸ About ▸ License names the licences and notices.
  • Source requests for the fan helper. The root fan helper is GPL-3.0-or-later and its complete source is public at Kkray19/LocalNook-fan-helper-source. Its Issues page is the contact for requests about that source; it is not a general support desk.

What doesn’t exist yet

  • A support email address or help desk
  • A published response-time commitment
  • Purchase help, order lookup and refund handling (there are no orders yet)

This page will be replaced with the real route before Oriel goes on sale.
