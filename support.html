Support

Help, honestly.

A general support route (somewhere you can write with any question and get a reply) is still an open item for launch. Privacy and License Agreement questions already have an address, below. Until a general route exists, this page says plainly what there is and what there isn’t.

What exists today

  • In the app. Settings ▸ Privacy lists the hosts Oriel may contact, shows how many requests it has made to each host since launch, and how the last one was answered. Settings ▸ About ▸ License names the licences and notices.
  • Privacy and License Agreement questions. oriel.nook@gmail.com answers them. For privacy questions and requests the Privacy Policy promises an answer without undue delay and within one month, or longer where the law allows.
  • Source requests for the fan helper. The root fan helper is GPL-3.0-or-later and its complete source is public at Kkray19/LocalNook-fan-helper-source. Its Issues page is the contact for requests about that source; it is not a general support desk.

What doesn’t exist yet

  • A help desk
  • A response-time commitment for other questions

This page will be replaced with the real route before the official download opens.
